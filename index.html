EARTH SYSTEM 000

Journey through astronomical scale

  1. Earth system. Earth is 12,742 kilometers in diameter. The Moon's mean distance is 384,400 kilometers.
  2. Inner Solar System. Earth orbits the Sun at one astronomical unit, or 149.6 million kilometers.
  3. Solar System. Neptune's mean distance from the Sun is 30.1 astronomical units.
  4. Stellar neighborhood. Proxima Centauri is 4.24 light-years away. Barnard's Star and Sirius provide wider reference points in the local stellar volume.
  5. Milky Way. The galaxy is approximately 100,000 light-years in diameter. The Sun is approximately 26,000 light-years from the galactic center.
  6. Local Group. The Andromeda Galaxy is approximately 2.5 million light-years away. The Large and Small Magellanic Clouds appear as nearby satellites of the Milky Way.
  7. Cosmic web. The Laniākea supercluster spans approximately 520 million light-years.
  8. Observable universe. The observable universe is approximately 92 billion light-years in diameter. Its horizon marks the limit of what can be observed, not a physical edge.

The illustrated positions and separations are compressed for legibility and do not form a literal spatial map.
