THE OBSERVATORY / FIELD NOTES

A change in perspective.

Cosmos Explorer is an invitation to look beyond familiar scales. Follow a camera through space, spend time with individual worlds, or explore the structures that surround our galaxy.

Choose how you explore.

The journey follows a fixed camera path. Scroll to move forward or backward, or choose Play flight for continuous travel. Moving the wheel pauses playback. Each featured world has an Explore link and a visible + marker.

The planet atlas and deep-space atlas are separate pages. Their observations stay in place until you choose another object.

Science and illustration.

Distances and planet sizes are composed for legibility. This is a cinematic interpretation, not a navigation map. Galaxies and the cosmic web are procedural illustrations.

Earth and Moon imagery: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio and LRO/LROC, Ernie Wright. Other globe maps: Solar System Scope, used under CC BY 4.0. Maps are applied to lit spheres; source colors are retained. Some unmapped terrain is artist reconstructed.

Planet atlas imagery comes from NASA mission reference pages. Physical parameters follow NASA/JPL. Rendering uses Three.js, distributed under the MIT license.
